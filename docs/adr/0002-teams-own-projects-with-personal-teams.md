# Teams own Projects; individuals get a Personal Team

DayStack will serve both individuals and small teams. Rather than keeping member-owned Projects alongside team-owned ones, every Project belongs to a Team, and each Member automatically receives a Personal Team of one. This gives one ownership, authorization, and Quota model instead of two parallel code paths, at the cost of migrating today's member-owned Projects into Personal Teams. Accepted as the direction for the teams-and-pricing release; the current solo MVP ships unchanged first.
